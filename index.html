About Me

A developer who cares about design, performance, and detail.

I'm Abdul Rabby, a Web Developer and WordPress Developer based in Kushtia, Bangladesh, working with Fleexstudio — a UI/UX and web design agency. My day-to-day work is building websites that look clean, load fast, and are easy for clients to manage.

My specialty is WordPress: custom theme development, Elementor and Elementor Pro builds, custom Elementor widget development, and detailed customization work that goes beyond what page builders offer out of the box. I also work with modern HTML, CSS, and JavaScript to build responsive interfaces that work smoothly on every device.

I enjoy the full process — turning a design into a working website, solving layout and performance problems, and polishing the small details that make a site feel professional. If you have a project in mind, I'd be glad to talk about it.
